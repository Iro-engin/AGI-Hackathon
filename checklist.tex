\section*{NeurIPS Paper Checklist}

\begin{enumerate}

\item \textbf{Claims: Do the main claims made in the abstract and introduction accurately reflect the paper's contributions and scope?}

\textbf{Answer: Yes.} The abstract and Section~1 state the strict one-step target, frozen-pilot scope, robust upper bound, conditional sub-Gaussian adaptation result, Gaussian rank-one lower bound, and the absence of empirical performance claims.

\item \textbf{Limitations: Does the paper discuss the limitations of the work performed by the authors?}

\textbf{Answer: Yes.} Section~\ref{sec:limitations} discusses the frozen coordinate system, one-sided correction, moment and dependence assumptions, the Gaussian scope of the minimax comparison, complete observations, computation, and the lack of empirical evidence.

\item \textbf{Theory, assumptions, and proofs: Did the authors state the full set of assumptions of all theoretical results and include complete proofs?}

\textbf{Answer: Yes.} Assumptions appear in or immediately before each theorem. Complete proofs and supporting lemmas appear in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}, including the weighted concentration radius, oracle inequality, H\"older rate, minimax lower bound, certificates, delay, and decision transfer.

\item \textbf{Experimental result reproducibility: If the contribution is a dataset or model, what steps were taken to make the results reproducible or verifiable?}

\textbf{Answer: Yes.} The contribution is a theoretical online algorithm rather than a trained model or dataset. Equations~\eqref{eq:weights}--\eqref{eq:forecast} specify the update, and Appendix~\ref{app:evaluation} specifies the information clock required to verify future implementations. No empirical result is used to support a main claim.

\item \textbf{Open access to data and code: If experiments were run, are the code, data, and instructions needed to reproduce the main experimental results included?}

\textbf{Answer: N/A.} The submission reports no experiments and uses no dataset.

\item \textbf{Experimental setting/details: If experiments were run, were all training and evaluation details specified?}

\textbf{Answer: N/A.} The submission reports no experiments.

\item \textbf{Experiment statistical significance: Does the paper report appropriate uncertainty or significance information for its experiments?}

\textbf{Answer: N/A.} The submission reports no experiments.

\item \textbf{Experiments compute resources: Does the paper provide sufficient information about the compute required for each experiment?}

\textbf{Answer: N/A.} The submission reports no experiments. The per-update computational complexity of the algorithm is stated in Section~\ref{sec:algorithm}.

\item \textbf{Code of Ethics: Have the authors read the NeurIPS Code of Ethics and ensured that the research conforms to it?}

\textbf{Answer: Yes.} The work is a mathematical and algorithmic study using no personal data, human subjects, or restricted assets, and the authors know of no conflict with the Code of Ethics.

\item \textbf{Broader impacts: If appropriate, does the paper discuss potential negative societal impacts?}

\textbf{Answer: Yes.} The Broader Impact section discusses decision instability, pilot misspecification, monitoring, and the limits of automated deployment.

\item \textbf{Safeguards: Are safeguards provided for releasing models with a high risk of misuse?}

\textbf{Answer: N/A.} The paper releases no pretrained model, generative system, scraped dataset, or other high-risk asset. It nevertheless describes pilot-validity monitoring and episode resets as deployment safeguards.

\item \textbf{Licenses: If existing assets are used, are their creators cited and their licenses and terms respected?}

\textbf{Answer: N/A.} No data, model, or third-party code asset is used in the scientific results. The manuscript uses the unmodified official NeurIPS 2026 style file for formatting and cites all research on which the analysis builds.

\item \textbf{Assets: If new assets are released, are they documented?}

\textbf{Answer: N/A.} The paper introduces no dataset, trained model, benchmark, or other research asset.

\item \textbf{Crowdsourcing and research with human subjects: If crowdsourcing or human-subject research was used, are instructions, compensation, and related details reported?}

\textbf{Answer: N/A.} The research uses no crowdsourcing or human subjects.

\item \textbf{IRB approvals: Were participant risks described and appropriate institutional approvals obtained, if applicable?}

\textbf{Answer: N/A.} The research uses no human participants or personally identifiable information.

\item \textbf{Declaration of LLM usage: Does the paper describe LLM usage when it forms an important, original, or non-standard component of the core research method?}

\textbf{Answer: N/A.} The core method, theoretical statements, and proofs do not use an LLM as a scientific component.
